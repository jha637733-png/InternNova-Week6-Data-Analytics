# Retail Sales Analysis: Insights and Recommendations


## 5 Key Insights

1. Sales Performance: Analyze overall sales performance and identify important business patterns.

2. Product Categories: Compare product categories to understand their contribution to sales.
3. Sales Trends: Examine sales over time to identify periods of higher or lower sales, if date information is available.

4. Data Quality: Check and remove duplicate records to improve the reliability of the analysis.

5. Relationships and Outliers: Use numeric distributions, correlation analysis, and boxplots to explore relationships and potential outliers.

## 2 Data-Driven Recommendations

1. Improve Sales Monitoring: Use the Power BI dashboard to monitor sales KPIs and compare performance across categories and time periods.
2. Use Data for Planning: Review category performance and investigate unusual values before making inventory, pricing, or promotional decisions.

## Conclusion

Python, Excel, and Power BI were used to explore the retail dataset, prepare visualizations, and support data-informed business decisions.
